— Our story

Born from friendship & shared tables.

ROSSO is a restaurant born from friendship and shared tables, created by a group of close friends united by a love for honest food, hospitality, and long, unforgettable moments around the table.

Rooted in the spirit of Italo-American family cooking, ROSSO is more than a restaurant — it is a living home where recipes are passed down, stories are shared, and everyone is treated like part of the family.

The menu celebrates generous, comforting dishes inspired by family recipes, made to be shared without rules. From slow-cooked classics to playful reinterpretations, every plate carries warmth, nostalgia, and intention. Alongside the food: good coffee, comforting desserts, signature cocktails, and a curated selection of beers.

Located in Seseh, away from the noise yet close to everything, ROSSO is where lunches turn into dinners, dinners turn into nights, and strangers often leave as friends.

"Italian soul,
Bali sun."
